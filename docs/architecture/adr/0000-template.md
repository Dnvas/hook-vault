# NNNN. Title in the form of a decision ("Use X for Y")

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** maintainer
- **Related:** issue #, stage N, supersedes NNNN (if any)

## Context

What is true about the system and the situation that makes a decision necessary. Facts, numbers, constraints (cost, time, skills, existing code). No options here.

## Options considered

1. **Option A.** One or two sentences on what it is. Pros. Cons.
2. **Option B.** Same.
3. **Option C.** Same, if there was one.

## Decision

The option chosen, in one sentence, and the two or three reasons that actually decided it.

## Consequences

- Easier: what this makes simpler or cheaper from now on.
- Harder: what this makes more difficult, what it rules out, what will need revisiting and when.
- Follow-ups: concrete work this creates (issues).
